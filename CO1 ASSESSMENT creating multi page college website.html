Why Choose SIMATS?

At SIMATS Engineering, we provide a holistic learning environment combining rigorous technical education with physical activities and career mentoring. Our modern computing labs and libraries ensure that resource-access is never a bottleneck for our students.

About Our Institution

Established in 2012, SIMATS Engineering has grown into a premier education hub for engineering and science. Our vision is to cultivate creative thinking and coding excellence to build engineering systems of the next generation.

We are accredited by leading agencies and sustain active collaborations with technological partners to offer internship scopes to our enrolled batch.

Academic Departments

We offer programs designed to provide academic depth and hands-on skill development:

Computer Science & Engineering

Focuses on programming paradigms, software development life cycle, algorithms, data structures, and database structures.

Web Technology & UI Design

Dedicated to front-end development, responsive styling using HTML/CSS, JavaScript frameworks, and browser performance optimization.

Information Technology

Covers computer networking, information security systems, XML database formats, cloud infrastructure, and network protocols.

Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

Introduces students to machine learning engines, mathematical analysis, data visualization, and statistics.
